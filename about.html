About ClearSpace Organizers

We're passionate about creating organized spaces that transform lives. Our mission is to bring clarity, calm, and functionality to every home we touch.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To transform chaotic spaces into organized sanctuaries that enhance quality of life, reduce stress, and promote sustainable living habits. We believe everyone deserves to live in a space that brings them joy and peace.

Vision

To be the leading professional organizing service that revolutionizes how people interact with their spaces, creating a world where everyone experiences the benefits of organized living.

Values

  • Empathy: We understand the emotional connection to belongings
  • Sustainability: We promote eco-friendly organizing practices
  • Education: We teach systems that last a lifetime
  • Respect: We honor your privacy and personal choices

Our Organizing Philosophy

The principles that guide every transformation we create

Personalized Approach

Every person and space is unique. We create customized solutions that work with your lifestyle, habits, and preferences.

Sustainable Systems

We don't just organize; we establish systems that are easy to maintain and evolve with your changing needs.

Eco-Conscious

We prioritize sustainable practices, responsible disposal, and environmentally friendly organizing solutions.

Non-Judgmental

We create a safe, supportive environment where you feel comfortable making decisions about your belongings.

Innovation

We stay current with the latest organizing trends, tools, and techniques to provide cutting-edge solutions.

Collaborative

We work alongside you, teaching and guiding rather than dictating. Your input is essential to the process.

Our Journey

How ClearSpace Organizers grew from a passion to a profession

2011

The Beginning

Founded by Sarah Mitchell, a passionate organizer who helped friends and family transform their spaces. The demand grew organically through word-of-mouth referrals.

2014

Professional Certification

Sarah became a Certified Professional Organizer (CPO) and expanded the team with like-minded professionals who shared the same vision and values.

2017

Service Expansion

Added specialized services including office organization, downsizing assistance, and virtual organizing sessions to serve a broader client base.

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched online organizing programs and virtual consultations, making professional organizing accessible to clients worldwide during the pandemic.

2023

Industry Recognition

Received the "Best Organizing Service" award and expanded to serve multiple cities with a team of 12 certified professional organizers.

2026

Continuing Excellence

Today, we continue to transform lives through organized spaces, maintaining our commitment to personalized service and sustainable practices.
