Our Step-by-Step Process

A systematic approach to lasting home transformation

1

Initial Consultation & Assessment

We begin with a comprehensive in-home consultation to understand your lifestyle, challenges, and goals. During this 90-minute session, we:

  • Tour your entire home and assess each space
  • Discuss your daily routines and pain points
  • Identify priority areas and quick wins
  • Take measurements and photos for planning
Duration: 90 minutes | Investment: Free with service package
2

Custom Organization Plan

Based on our assessment, we create a detailed organization plan tailored to your specific needs and preferences:

  • Room-by-room organization strategy
  • Custom storage solutions and product recommendations
  • Timeline and project milestones
  • Budget considerations and shopping list
Duration: 3-5 business days | Delivery: Digital and printed copy
3

Decluttering & Sorting

This is where the transformation begins. We work alongside you to sort through every item systematically:

  • Gentle, non-judgmental decision-making process
  • Sort items into Keep, Donate, Sell, and Recycle categories
  • Coordinate donation pickups and recycling
  • Document valuable items for tax purposes
Duration: Varies by space size | Team: 1-2 organizers
4

Organization & System Implementation

With the decluttering complete, we implement your custom organization systems:

  • Install storage solutions and organizers
  • Create zones for different activities
  • Implement labeling and categorizing systems
  • Arrange items for maximum accessibility
Duration: 1-3 days per room | Includes: All organizing supplies
5

Education & Maintenance Plan

We ensure you can maintain your newly organized space with confidence:

  • Walkthrough of all new systems and routines
  • Custom maintenance checklist for each room
  • Tips for family involvement and habits
  • Schedule follow-up sessions if needed
Duration: 60 minutes | Includes: Digital maintenance guide
